## How to become a member

Joining Rotary usually happens through a referral from a current member, but we encourage you to reach out directly if you're interested — we'll connect you with a club member.

1. **Get in touch** — write to us via [contact](/en/contact) or join us at a meeting as a guest.
2. **Get to know the club** — attend a few meetings and events to see whether our values and activities are a good fit.
3. **Proposal** — a current member formally proposes you for membership.
4. **Board approval** — the club's board reviews and approves the proposal.
5. **Induction ceremony** — you officially become a member of Rotary Club Caransebeș and the wider Rotary International family.

### Requirements

There are no restrictions based on gender, ethnicity, religion or background. We do ask for a genuine commitment to Rotary values and a willingness to actively take part in club life — meetings, projects and events.

Have questions? [Contact us](/en/contact) — we're happy to help.
