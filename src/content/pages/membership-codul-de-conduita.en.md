title: "Code of conduct — Rotary Club Caransebeș"
description: "The Rotary International code of conduct, upheld by every member of Rotary Club Caransebeș."
---

As a Rotary member, I will:

1. Act with integrity and high ethical standards in my personal and professional life.
2. Deal fairly with others and treat them and their occupations with respect.
3. Use my professional experience and expertise to serve others in need through Rotary programs, projects and activities.
4. Avoid behavior that reflects adversely on Rotary or other Rotarians.
5. Help maintain a harassment-free environment in Rotary meetings, events and activities, reporting any suspected harassment to the appropriate club or district authorities.

This code reflects our shared commitment to Rotary's core values: **integrity, diversity, service and leadership**.
