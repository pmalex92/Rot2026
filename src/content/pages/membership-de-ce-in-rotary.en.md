## A community of leaders determined to do good

Rotary brings together business people, professionals and community leaders who believe actions matter more than words. Here's what you gain as a member:

- **Real impact** — you take part in concrete projects with visible results in the community.
- **Connections** — you connect with professionals across every field, in Romania and worldwide.
- **Personal and professional growth** — leadership opportunities, training and international networking.
- **Lasting friendships** — built on shared values: integrity, service, respect.
- **A global network** — over 46,000 Rotary and Rotaract clubs in more than 200 countries and regions.

If you want to turn your time and expertise into real change for the Caransebeș community, [find out how to join our club](/en/membership/admitere-in-club/).
