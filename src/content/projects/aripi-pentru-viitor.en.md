We have launched **"Wings for the Future"** (*Aripi pentru Viitor*) – a project through which we want to invest, for the long term, in education and in the future of young people with excellent results.

This year, **3 deserving young people** will receive support throughout their high school studies, **for 4 years**.

## More than financial support

We want to stand by them throughout high school, so that their family's financial means never become an obstacle to their education and their plans.

Depending on each pupil's needs, the support will cover:

- essential school expenses and supplies;
- transport and accommodation;
- IT equipment;
- mentoring and guidance for the future.

We believe education can change a life. And sometimes, for a young person to follow their path, all it takes is someone who believes in them and stands by them. "Wings for the Future" is about giving these young people the chance to build their own future.

## Would you like to help a young person follow their dream?

You can support "Wings for the Future" and contribute to the 4 years of high school of a pupil who needs help to continue their education. Every contribution is one more chance: for education, for growth and for a future built through their own efforts.

You will find the donation details on the [Donate](/en/doneaza) page — please use the reference "Aripi pentru Viitor". For any details, [write to us](/en/contact) or send us a private message on [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/RotaryClubCaransebes).

**Together we can give wings to dreams that deserve to fly.**
