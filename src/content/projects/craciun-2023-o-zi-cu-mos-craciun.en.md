In partnership with **Caransebeș City Hall** and the **"George Suru" House of Culture**, the Rotary Club organised the interactive show **"A day with Santa Claus"**, attended by **more than 400 children**.

Through **Christmas for Everyone**, Interact Caransebeș handed out gifts to the children of the Balta Sărată kindergarten, while the Rotary seniors gave sweets to the primary pupils of Balta Sărată School No. 1.

Also in December, donations from hot drink sales at the club's **"Little House"** went to Rotary projects.
