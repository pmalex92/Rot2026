In 2025, Rotary Club Caransebeș launched the **"A helping hand for little souls"** campaign to equip the Paediatric Psychiatry ward and improve conditions for the children being treated there.

In 2026 the campaign resulted in the **donation of a refrigerator and 8 bedside tables** to the ward — concrete support for the children's comfort and for the medical staff's work.
