As part of the district project **"Education for the new generation"**, dedicated to rural schools:

- **2022** — a printer was donated to the **Armeniș secondary school**.
- **2023** — the club donated **three smart boards** to the schools in **Armeniș, Băuțar and Rusca Montană**.
