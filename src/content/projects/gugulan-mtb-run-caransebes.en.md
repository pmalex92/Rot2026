Rotary Club Caransebeș supports **Gugulan MTB & RUN Caransebeș**, a race that has already become a tradition.

- **2022** — the club supported the sports event dedicated to movement and active living, held in Teiuș Park.
- **2024** — financial support for the race, promoting an active lifestyle and sporting achievement.
- **2025** — presence, sponsorship and fundraising at the event, promoting movement, community and solidarity.
