Meetings in the **summer of 2023** with **Rotary Club Erbach-Michelstadt** (Germany) opened a new chapter of collaboration in the social field.

On **10 April 2024**, together with the president of Rotary Club Erbach-Michelstadt, we planted the **Romanian-German Rotary friendship tree** — a symbolic gesture of continuity and international cooperation.
