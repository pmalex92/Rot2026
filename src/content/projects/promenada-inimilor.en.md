The **Promenade of Hearts** is one of Rotary Club Caransebeș's recurring actions, dedicated to health and movement.

- **2015** — the first Promenade of Hearts.
- **2018** — the event brought the community together to promote an active lifestyle, responsibility for one's own health and civic involvement.
- **2022** — the project continued, focusing on cardiovascular health and the importance of exercise.
- **2025** — a new edition reaffirming the club's commitment to prevention and an active lifestyle.
