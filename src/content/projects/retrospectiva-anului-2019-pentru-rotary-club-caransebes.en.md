2019 was one of the most important years in the club's history:

- **February 2019** — continuing the [Global Grant](/en/proiecte/global-grant-spitalul-municipal-caransebes/) project, **two truckloads of furniture and medical equipment** were donated to Caransebeș Hospital.
- **21 February 2019** — a **Storz bronchoscope** worth **148,000 lei** was handed over.
- **"Supporting the present for the future"** — the club continued to support education and achievement by rewarding olympiad pupils and athletes with outstanding results.

Between 2017 and 2019, Rotary Club Caransebeș consolidated long-standing projects and carried forward initiatives with real and lasting impact on the community.
