Protecting the environment is one of the club's constant priorities.

- **March 2023** — two afforestation actions, **Rotary Plants Trees**, continued the club's commitment to the environment and to local partnerships.
- **2025** — the club took part in clean-up and planting actions and in projects that highlighted the local community and places of significance for the town.
