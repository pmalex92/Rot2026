On **23 February 2024**, **Interact Caransebeș**, supported by Rotary Club Caransebeș, organised the **Interact Super Ball**, a charity event that raised **7,500 lei for Cristi**, a child diagnosed with spastic tetraparesis.

In the same context, **12,000 lei went to the Caransebeș Childhood Autism Association**.
