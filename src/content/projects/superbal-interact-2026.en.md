**SuperBal 2026**, organised by **Interact Caransebeș** and supported by Rotary Club Caransebeș, brought together young people, energy and solidarity in another defining moment for the generation carrying the Rotary spirit forward.

The Interact Super Ball is an annual event that supports important cases in the community. [Learn more about Interact Caransebeș](/en/despre-noi/interact).
