In partnership with **Rotary Club Reșița** and the **Zoly Kovacs International Foundation**, the club supported the **"Young people must know"** project.

The project donated books to **27 high schools in Caraș-Severin County** and organised educational meetings between pupils and the book's author — an investment in historical memory and in the formation of the young generation.
