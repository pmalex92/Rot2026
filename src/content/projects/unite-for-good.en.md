Rotary Club Caransebeș answered the **humanitarian appeal launched by Rotary Club Fălticeni** with a donation of **2,000 lei** for communities affected by extreme weather.

The club also joined the **"Together for Adriana"** campaign, contributing **€1,000** towards her treatment.
